To Dainne my loml,

I have always been better at understanding things than explaining what I feel. I can organize thoughts, find patterns, and make sense of complicated things, but somehow, when it comes to you, even the simplest words feel insufficient.

I love you—not only because of how you make me feel, but because of who you are. Your kindness, your little habits, your laughter, and the way you make ordinary moments feel worth remembering. You made me feel accepted in moments when I struggled to accept myself. You reminded me that I could be cared for without having to prove that I deserved it.

And I miss you. I miss our conversations, the random stories, the quiet moments, and the ordinary days that became special simply because you were part of them. Even Grizzly reminds me of you. It is funny how an ordinary plush can hold so many memories, but I guess that is what happens when someone becomes part of the little details of your life.

Do you remember when we got on the wrong train? And when I spilled the Coke? I swear, NATAPON LAHAT 'YUN. 😭 It was such a mess, but seeing you laugh made the whole thing feel worth it. I think about moments like that more often than you might expect. They were not grand or carefully planned. They were simply ours, and that made them special.

I also owe you an apology. I know there were moments when I fell short—when I did not listen well enough, when my pride got in the way, or when I became too focused on explaining my side instead of understanding yours. I never want my intentions to become an excuse for the ways I may have hurt you. I am sorry for the times I made things heavier when I should have been more thoughtful.

You know, there were so many times when I wanted to cry in front of you, but I was too shy to let it happen. I would try so hard to contain everything, as if you would somehow see me differently for being vulnerable. The funny part is that I was already struggling to hold it all in. 😭 I wish I had been less afraid of letting you see that side of me. Tapos, yun- I was really hoping na sana, hanggang dulo, tayo pa rin. That we would fulfill our dreams together, get married, and finally live the future we once imagined. I used to picture Say You Won’t Let Go by James Arthur playing as you walked down the aisle, while I stood there, unable to hold back my tears as I watched you walk towards me. I wanted to see you coming closer, knowing that after everything, we finally made it. I genuinely wanted that future with you.

I admire you as your own person, beyond what you have done for me or what you have given me. You have your own dreams, your own thoughts, and your own life to live. I hope you continue choosing the things that bring you peace and help you become the person you want to be. You deserve care, patience, and happiness—not only from other people, but from yourself, too.

I cannot fit everything I feel into one page, and maybe that is okay. I just wanted you to know that I am grateful for the time, the memories, the laughter, and the love we shared. I do not expect this letter to fix everything or ask anything from you. I only wanted to be honest about what has always mattered to me.

I love you. More than these paragraphs can explain, and more sincerely than I have sometimes known how to show.

With all my heart, — D.

P.S. The Coke incident is still one of the funniest disasters in my personal history.
